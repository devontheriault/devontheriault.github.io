CF Timer Support

A workout timer that looks and sounds like a gym wall timer.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas: dev.theriault@gmail.com. Please include your device model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Restoring Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your App Store account. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Pro doesn't transfer between iPhone and Android.

Beeps when the app is in the background

CF Timer uses notifications to beep at interval changes while you're in another app or the screen is locked. If you don't hear them, check that notifications are allowed for CF Timer in your device's Settings and that the ringer isn't muted.
