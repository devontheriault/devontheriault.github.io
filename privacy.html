CF Timer Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

CF Timer is a workout timer. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. This policy explains the little data that is involved and who handles it.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, your last used timer settings, your Saved Workouts and any workout in progress are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Background beeps use local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service is used.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads.

  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice under Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the app.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Buying Pro removes all ads, and no ad requests are made after that.

Google's use of this data is covered by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Purchases

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The purchase is verified by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history so it can unlock and restore Pro. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't build profiles of you, and we don't collect health or fitness data from your device.

Children

CF Timer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
